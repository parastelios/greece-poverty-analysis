# ESS life-satisfaction aggregates

These values come from the official European Social Survey Data Portal's
public Analysis tab for `stflife` (life satisfaction, 0-10). The portal applies
the post-stratification weight including the design weight. Raw respondent data
remain login-gated.

For each round, the displayed weighted percentage in each response category
was multiplied by its score and summed to recover an approximate country mean.
The approximation reflects the precision displayed by the portal. The linked
analysis URLs provide the authoritative distributions and edition provenance.

`greece_rank_worst` ranks the lowest mean as 1. The all-country comparison uses
every country displayed in that round. The balanced sensitivity uses the 12
countries present in all six Greek rounds: Belgium, Switzerland, Finland,
France, United Kingdom, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Netherlands, Norway, Portugal,
and Slovenia.

The series must not be spliced to Eurostat's EU-SILC life-satisfaction series.
ESS provides two pre-crisis rounds, observations around the onset and early
crisis, and then an approximately decade-long gap before Round 10. It cannot
show the crisis trough or a continuous trajectory.
